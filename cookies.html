BROWSER STORAGE + EXTERNAL SERVICES

Cookies and third parties

This notice explains the limited browser storage and third-party content used by the current Tires 4 Less website build.

Local request fallback

If a request cannot be verified as delivered, the site may save a limited copy in browser local storage under t4l_request_fallback. This is a local browser copy, not a server inbox, and it can be cleared through browser settings. Do not store payment or account information in a request form.

Essential website operation

The site uses ordinary browser behavior for navigation, form status messages, request timing protection and responsive controls. No account-login cookie is required to browse the website.

Maps and financing providers

Location pages may load Google Maps. Finance buttons and QR links open American First Finance or Snap Finance. Those providers may use their own cookies or tracking technologies under their own notices and terms. Tires 4 Less does not control those practices.

Analytics and advertising

No Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager or advertising measurement ID is activated in this build. Any future measurement or advertising technology should be added only after the owner approves the account, disclosures and user-choice approach.

Browser choices

You can clear local storage, block cookies or change browser permissions through your browser settings. Blocking storage may affect request fallback or third-party content, but you can still call a Tires 4 Less location directly.

Questions and updates

Contact tirewebdev4life@gmail.com with questions about browser storage or third-party links. The updated date above identifies the latest version of this notice.

Third-party provider terms and privacy notices control when you leave the Tires 4 Less website.
