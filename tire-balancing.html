SERVICE DETAILS

BALANCING VS. WHEEL ALIGNMENT.

Tire Balancing addresses weight differences in the tire-and-wheel assembly that can contribute to vibration. Wheel Alignment is a separate service that deals with wheel angles and is currently offered at Freedom Drive only. A vehicle can have a balancing concern, an Alignment concern, another mechanical issue, or more than one issue at the same time, so the symptom still matters.

SERVICE DETAILS

WHAT TO TELL THE SHOP ABOUT VIBRATION.

Include when you notice the vibration, whether the tires were recently installed or rotated, and any other tire or steering symptoms. That information does not diagnose the vehicle online, but it gives the shop a better starting point. Choose the shop first, then request Tire Balancing; if Alignment becomes relevant, the Alignment path routes to Freedom Drive.

SERVICE CONTEXT

WHEN THE VIBRATION STARTED CAN HELP.

Tell the shop whether vibration began after a tire installation, rotation, impact or other recent change, and whether it happens at a particular speed range. That information does not diagnose the cause online, but it gives the technician useful context before the vehicle is checked.

Balancing is not the same as Alignment. If the concern includes pulling, steering position or uneven wear, the shop may recommend a different inspection path. Wheel Alignment itself is currently available only at Freedom Drive.

COMMON QUESTIONS

TIRE BALANCING QUESTIONS.

What does Tire Balancing address?

Balancing addresses weight differences in the tire-and-wheel assembly that can contribute to vibration.

Is Tire Balancing the same as Wheel Alignment?

No. Wheel Alignment is a separate service and is currently offered at Freedom Drive only.

Does vibration always mean the tires need balancing?

No. Vibration can have different causes, so the specific vehicle may still need inspection.

Can balancing be coordinated with Tire Installation?

Yes. Tire Balancing can be requested with installation when needed.
