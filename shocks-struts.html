SERVICE CONTEXT

RIDE QUALITY IS THE STARTING CLUE.

Excessive bouncing, a rough or unsettled ride and visible component concerns are reasons to have the suspension checked, but they do not identify the exact part by themselves. Include when the behavior happens, whether it changed recently and any other suspension or steering symptoms.

Shocks & Struts sits inside the broader Suspension & Steering service family. If the shop finds a different suspension issue, the service path can expand from there. Alignment remains a separate Freedom Drive-only service when wheel-angle adjustment is actually needed.
