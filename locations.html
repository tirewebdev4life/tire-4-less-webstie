LOCATIONS FAQ

LOCATIONS & HOURS QUESTIONS.

Quick answers about Locations Hub, based on the current Tires 4 Less service and location rules.

How many Tires 4 Less locations are there?

There are three physical shops: Freedom Drive and North Tryon in Charlotte, plus Concord on Cabarrus Ave W.

Which location is open 24/7?

Freedom Drive is open 24/7. North Tryon is open Monday–Thursday 7:30 AM–9:00 PM and Friday–Sunday until midnight. Concord is open 7:30 AM–9:00 PM daily.

Does every location offer Wheel Alignment?

No. Wheel Alignment is currently available at Freedom Drive only.

Is 24/7 Roadside Assistance tied to shop hours?

No. Roadside Assistance is a separate 24-hour service at (980) 319-0381.

How should I choose a shop?

Start with location, hours and the service you need. Use the branch pages for direct phone, directions and the branch-specific request path.
