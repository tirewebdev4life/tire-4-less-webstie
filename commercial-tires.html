COMMERCIAL TIRE QUESTIONS

COMMERCIAL TIRE & FLEET QUESTIONS.

Quick answers about Fleet & Commercial, based on the current Tires 4 Less service and location rules.

What commercial vehicles can I start a tire request for?

Use the commercial request for semi-trucks, box trucks and other commercial vehicles; send the vehicle type, tire size and quantity so the shop can confirm the right path.

What commercial services are confirmed?

Fleet Tire Service, Commercial Truck Fleet Tire Service and Commercial Truck Basic Maintenance & Services are confirmed in the current service catalog.

Do you provide heavy or diesel repair?

Heavy/Diesel repair is not listed as a standard service. Call with the vehicle and work needed so the team can confirm the current path.

Do you offer commercial Roadside service?

For commercial Roadside needs, call (980) 319-0381 with the vehicle and current location so the team can confirm available help.

What information should a fleet or truck request include?

Vehicle type, tire size, quantity, preferred location, contact details and any basic-maintenance context the shop should know.
