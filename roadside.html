ROADSIDE QUESTIONS

24/7 ROADSIDE QUESTIONS.

Quick answers about Roadside Assistance, based on the current Tires 4 Less service and location rules.

Is Tires 4 Less Roadside Assistance available 24 hours?

Yes. 24/7 Roadside Assistance uses (980) 319-0381.

What Roadside services are currently confirmed?

Emergency Fuel Delivery / Gas Delivery, Roadside Tire Service, Mobile Tire Installation, Jump Starts and Vehicle Lockout Service are confirmed Roadside options.

What should I tell the Roadside team?

Your current location, vehicle year/make/model, what happened and whether the vehicle can move.

What areas does Roadside cover?

Exact geographic coverage is not published. Call with your current location so the team can confirm availability.

Are the physical Tires 4 Less shops all open 24 hours?

Freedom Drive is open 24/7; North Tryon is open Mon–Thu 7:30 AM–9:00 PM and Fri–Sun until midnight. Concord is open 7:30 AM–9:00 PM daily.

What if Roadside cannot solve the problem?

The next step may be Towing, which is a separate vehicle-movement path.
