COMMON QUESTIONS

TIRES 4 LESS SERVICE QUESTIONS.

Quick answers about All Services, based on the current Tires 4 Less service and location rules.

What services does Tires 4 Less offer?

The confirmed catalog includes New and Used Tires, Tire Installation, Rotation, Balancing, Flat Repair, TPMS, Wheel Alignment, Mechanic Services, Auto Glass, Fleet & Commercial service, Rim Repair, 24/7 Roadside Assistance and Towing.

Where is Wheel Alignment available?

Wheel Alignment is currently available at Freedom Drive only.

How do I request planned shop service?

Choose the shop first, then select the service, vehicle and preferred date/time. The requested time is not confirmed until the shop responds.

Should I use the appointment form for Roadside Assistance?

No. Roadside is a separate 24/7 phone-first path at (980) 319-0381.

What if I am not sure which service I need?

Choose Other / Not Sure in the Shop Service request and describe the vehicle symptoms.
