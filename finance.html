Financing questions

WHAT TO KNOW BEFORE APPLYING.

Does applying confirm that a tire is available?

No. Submit a separate Tire Availability request so Tires 4 Less can confirm current options and pricing.

Does financing confirm my appointment?

No. Requested dates and times remain preferences until the selected shop responds.

Does Tires 4 Less decide approval?

No. Each provider controls its own application, approval decision, terms and disclosures.

Is Snap a traditional loan?

The supplied Snap material describes the option as a lease-to-own agreement provided by Snap RTO LLC.
