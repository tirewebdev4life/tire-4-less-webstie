NEW TIRE QUESTIONS

NEW TIRE QUESTIONS.

Quick answers about New Tires, based on the current Tires 4 Less service and location rules.

Which new-tire brands does Tires 4 Less carry or source?

New tire brand requests include Michelin, Continental, Cooper, Mickey Thompson, Goodyear and BFGoodrich. Availability varies by size and location and is confirmed by the shop.

How do I check new-tire availability?

Send your tire size or vehicle, quantity, preferred brand and preferred shop. The team confirms the actual options.

Do you show live new-tire inventory online?

Not currently. Send the tire size, quantity, preferred brand and shop, and Tires 4 Less will confirm the current new-tire options.

Can I request installation and balancing with new tires?

Yes. Tire Installation and Tire Balancing are separate confirmed service options that can be coordinated with the tire request.

Do you service TPMS sensors?

Yes. TPMS Sensor Replacement, Relearn & Programming are confirmed Tire Services.
