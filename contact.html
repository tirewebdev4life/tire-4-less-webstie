COMMON QUESTIONS

CONTACT & REQUEST QUESTIONS.

Quick answers about Contact / Requests, based on the current Tires 4 Less service and location rules.

24-HOUR ROADSIDE: (980) 319-0381 →
What is the difference between Tire Availability and Shop Service?

Tire Availability checks current tire options by size/quantity/location. Shop Service is for planned in-shop work such as tire service, mechanic work or Rim Repair.

Why is my email required?

The request system needs an email so Tires 4 Less can respond and send you a copy of what you submitted.

Does submitting a preferred date/time book an appointment?

No. The requested date/time is not confirmed until the shop responds.

Does a tire request reserve or guarantee inventory?

No. The request starts an availability check; the shop confirms actual options.

How do I request Auto Glass?

Use the Auto Glass path for windshield replacement or chip repair, and choose In Shop or Mobile.

How do I get 24/7 Roadside Assistance?

Call (980) 319-0381. Roadside is separate from planned appointment forms.
