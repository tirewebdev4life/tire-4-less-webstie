SERVICE DETAILS

BRAKE SYMPTOMS START THE CONVERSATION — INSPECTION DECIDES THE WORK.

Noise, vibration, pedal changes or a change in stopping response are reasons to have the brake system checked. Brake pads, rotors and other brake components have to be evaluated on the specific vehicle before Tires 4 Less can confirm the needed work.

SERVICE DETAILS

WHAT TO INCLUDE IN A BRAKE REQUEST.

Send the vehicle year, make and model, the symptoms you notice and when they occur. Choose the Tires 4 Less shop you want to visit and send a preferred date/time. If the concern turns out to involve another system, the mechanic-service hub can connect you to Suspension & Steering, Shocks & Struts or another appropriate path.

SERVICE CONTEXT

THE SYMPTOM HELPS — THE INSPECTION DECIDES.

Brake noise, vibration and pedal changes can point to several different causes. The purpose of the request is to get the vehicle and symptoms in front of the shop, not to decide online that pads or rotors must be replaced. Include when the symptom happens and whether it recently changed.

If the vehicle also has pulling, steering or suspension concerns, note those separately so the shop can decide whether another mechanic-service path is relevant. Keeping the request specific makes it easier to route the vehicle to the right next step.

COMMON QUESTIONS

BRAKE & ROTOR QUESTIONS.

What are common reasons to have the brakes checked?

Brake noise, vibration, a change in pedal feel or a change in stopping response are reasons to request an inspection.

Does every brake service require new rotors?

No. The shop needs to inspect the specific brake system before recommending pads, rotors or other work.

Can the request diagnose the brake problem?

No. The request routes the concern, but the vehicle still needs inspection.

What should I include in the request?

Vehicle year, make and model, symptoms, preferred location and preferred date/time.
