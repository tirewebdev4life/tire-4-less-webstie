SERVICE DETAILS

ALIGNMENT, BALANCING AND SUSPENSION ARE NOT THE SAME THING.

Pulling, an off-center steering wheel or uneven tire wear can be reasons to have Alignment checked, but those symptoms can also involve tires, steering or suspension. Tire Balancing addresses the tire-and-wheel assembly; Suspension & Steering Repair addresses mechanical components. The specific vehicle still needs inspection before the shop can identify the actual cause and recommend the next step.

SERVICE CONTEXT

FREEDOM DRIVE IS THE ALIGNMENT DESTINATION.

Alignment requests should point directly to 1627 Freedom Drive, Charlotte, NC 28208 rather than asking customers to choose among all three shops. Freedom Drive is the single Tires 4 Less Alignment location, so Alignment requests should start there.

If the symptoms suggest broader suspension or steering concerns, use the Suspension & Steering Repair path as well. Alignment may be part of the overall diagnosis, but it should not replace an inspection of mechanical components when those symptoms are present.
