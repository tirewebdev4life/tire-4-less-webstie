USED TIRE QUESTIONS

USED TIRE QUESTIONS.

Quick answers about Used Tires, based on the current Tires 4 Less service and location rules.

How do I check used-tire availability?

Send the tire size, quantity and preferred Tires 4 Less location. Used inventory changes frequently, so the shop confirms current options before you visit.

Do you publish used-tire inventory online?

No. Used tires remain request-based because the available tires change too frequently for a reliable public stock list.

Can I request installation with a used tire?

Yes. Tire Installation is a separate service path that can be coordinated after the tire option is confirmed.

What if I do not know my tire size?

Send the vehicle year, make and model or use the tire-size information on the sidewall as a starting point.

What warranty comes with a used tire?

Ask the shop for the current used-tire warranty terms before purchase so the duration, exclusions and claim process are clear.
