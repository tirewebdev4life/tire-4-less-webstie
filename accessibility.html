ACCESS FOR EVERY CUSTOMER

Accessibility statement

Tires 4 Less is working to make this website usable with keyboards, assistive technology, mobile devices and larger text settings. This statement describes the current approach and does not make a blanket conformance claim.

Current accessibility practices

  • Skip-to-content links, semantic page structure and visible keyboard focus.
  • Form labels, required-field cues and status messages that can be announced by assistive technology.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images and hidden decorative icons.
  • Responsive layouts, readable mobile sizing and high-contrast controls.
  • Call, directions and request paths that remain available without relying on a mouse.

Third-party content

Google Maps, American First Finance and Snap Finance content or destinations are controlled by their providers. Their interfaces may have separate accessibility features or limitations. Location, team and completed-work photo areas remain reserved for approved Tires 4 Less material and are not customer-proof claims.

Request an accommodation

If a page, map or form is difficult to use, call the selected location or email tirewebdev4life@gmail.com. Include the page address and a short description of the barrier, but do not send payment or account information. Tires 4 Less can provide another contact path when available.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility feedback is used to prioritize fixes for keyboard operation, text readability, contrast, form completion, mobile layouts and alternative ways to reach the business. This page will be updated when the website accessibility approach changes.

For immediate roadside assistance, call (980) 319-0381. Physical shop hours vary by location.
