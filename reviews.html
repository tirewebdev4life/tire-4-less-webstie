REVIEWS & OUR WORK QUESTIONS.

Quick answers about Reviews / Our Work, based on the current Tires 4 Less service and location rules.

Are the customer reviews verified?

Customer feedback on this page is shown only when its source has been verified.

Can reviews be filtered by location?

Yes, but only when the review or job record actually identifies Freedom Drive, North Tryon or Concord.

Will star ratings or review totals be shown?

We display ratings or review totals only when the source can be verified.

Can work photos appear without a customer review?

Yes. A real work record can stand on its own if the service and imagery are factual and no testimonial is fabricated.
