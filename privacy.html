HOW INFORMATION IS HANDLED

Privacy notice

This notice describes the information the Tires 4 Less website may receive when you request tires, service, Auto Glass, Roadside help or Towing.

Information you choose to send

Forms may ask for your name, email address, phone number, selected location, requested service, vehicle or tire-size details, preferred date or time, current vehicle location, destination and notes. Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, government identification numbers or other sensitive information through a website form.

How requests are used

Request information is used to respond to you, check the requested tire or service path, coordinate with the selected location, send a customer acknowledgement and help prevent duplicate or abusive submissions. A request is not an appointment, inventory confirmation, price quote or service-zone confirmation until Tires 4 Less responds.

Request delivery and records

Website requests are routed through the configured Google Apps Script web-app endpoint. The request may be emailed to tirewebdev4life@gmail.com, an acknowledgement may be sent to the email address you provide, and a private request-log row may be created for business follow-up. Access to request records should be limited to authorized Tires 4 Less personnel.

Browser storage and third parties

If delivery cannot be verified, the site may save a limited copy in this browser’s local storage under t4l_request_fallback so a local test or request is not silently lost. Google Maps may load on location pages. Financing links open American First Finance or Snap Finance pages. Those providers have separate privacy notices and terms, and Tires 4 Less does not control their data practices.

Retention, security and choices

Request information may be retained as reasonably needed to respond, operate the business, maintain appropriate records, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Do not rely on a browser fallback as a business inbox. You may clear local browser storage through your browser settings and may contact Tires 4 Less to ask about a request associated with your email address.

Children and changes

The website is intended for general customers and is not directed to children. Tires 4 Less may update this notice when website practices change; the updated date above will identify the latest posted version.

Privacy questions

Contact tirewebdev4life@gmail.com with a privacy question or request.

Do not include private financial, account-login or payment information in a Tires 4 Less website request.
